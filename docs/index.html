Three moves, left to right

The board is the method. There is nothing else to learn.

1

Brain dump

Type everything on your mind and press Enter. Don't sort, don't schedule. Just get it out of your head.

2

Today's 3

Drag in the three tasks that would make the day a good one. Smaller things go to "Also today".

3

Timebox

Drag tasks onto the timeline. Meetings from your calendar are locked blocks, so the plan fits the time you have.

Dark or light

Dark is the standard look. One button in the top bar switches to light, and the planner remembers it.

Small on purpose

A planner should take a minute in the morning, not become another project to manage.

  • Your calendar on the timelineGoogle Calendar events show as striped blocks you can't plan over. The header tells you how much of the day is still free.
  • Time blocks back to your calendarOne button writes the day's plan to a separate "Planner" calendar. Send it again and it replaces, never duplicates.
  • Nothing gets lostWhat you didn't finish returns to the brain dump the next day, marked "carried".
  • Projects in colorGive each project a color and see at a glance where the day is going.
  • Drag, drop, resize, undoBlocks snap to 15 minutes. Pull the bottom edge to change the length. Deleted by mistake? Undo.
  • Subtasks and notesOpen a task to give it a checklist and a notes box. The task shows how far along you are.
  • BoardsSeparate lists for separate people or areas of work, one button each. Drag a task onto a button to move it.
  • Two things at onceTasks can share a time on the timeline; they sit side by side.
  • Dark and vividDark by default. One button switches to a light look.
  • Your data stays with youTasks live in one file on your machine. No analytics, nothing loaded from other sites.
  • Nothing to maintainPlain PHP and plain JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step, no database server.

Run it on your computer

You need PHP, and nothing else. The planner opens in your browser, with no sign-up and no sign-in.

Windows

Download the ZIP, unzip it, and double-click start.bat. If PHP is missing, it offers to install it for you.

macOS

brew install php
git clone https://github.com/ToniHaddad/planner
cd planner
./start.sh

Linux

sudo apt install php-cli php-sqlite3 \
  php-curl php-mbstring
git clone https://github.com/ToniHaddad/planner
cd planner
./start.sh

Work on more than one computer? Put the same app on any web host that runs PHP, behind a username and password, and open it from anywhere. Hosting guide

Connect Google Calendar in three minutes

The planner walks you through it, step by step. It never sees your Google password.

  1. Copy a small script from the planner's settings.
  2. Paste it into your own Google account, in Google Apps Script.
  3. Deploy it and allow it to read your calendar.
  4. Paste the address Google gives you back into the planner.
